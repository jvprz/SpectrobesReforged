# Spectrobes Reforged 0.6.0-beta.1

Our first beta brings a Survival route into the current exploration and Krawl encounter systems.

## New Survival crafting

- Craft the white Prizmod with amethyst shards, a glass pane, iron, gold and copper.
- Craft the incubator with glass, iron, a calibrated sculk sensor, gold and copper.
- Recolor any Prizmod with any of the sixteen dyes.
- Discover the equipment recipes through the recipe book.

## Krawl vortex encounters

- Discover rare small vortices on the Overworld surface.
- Vortices slowly pursue nearby players and trigger an encounter on contact, regardless of the held item.
- The vortex dissolves and three Blova appear around the caught player.
- Defeat all three to receive two random basic minerals, dropped in front of you.
- Your scanner companion stays safely stored during the battle and returns afterward with its saved data.
- Encounters save their progress, have time and distance limits, and clean up cancelled enemies.
- Improved funnel visuals, subtle particles, terrain following, smoother movement, battle sounds and translated messages.

## Advancements and documentation

- Ten advancements covering the implemented systems, including Complete a Vortex and awakening all six supported species.
- Advancement text and battle subtitles in all six supported languages.
- Player wiki with equipment recipes, advancement requirements and a vortex guide.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**
- **NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer** for Minecraft 1.21.1
- **GeckoLib 4.8.3 or newer** for NeoForge 1.21.1 (required dependency)
- **Java 21**

## Still in development

This is a beta of exploration and Krawl encounters. Evolution, Spectrobe adult-team combat, large Krawl nests, and fossil analysis/cleaning are not complete. Six species currently have awakening definitions; other fossil items remain collectible.

Back up your world before updating. Replace the previous mod JAR rather than keeping both versions installed.
