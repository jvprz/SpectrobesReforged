# Companion Life — private playtest

Version: **0.7.1-beta.1-living-playtest**. This is an experimental build for feedback, not a published release. The stable version number in gradle.properties remains unchanged; this JAR is built with a version override.

## What to try

1. Equip a Child in the Prizmod and stand still on open, level ground for 20–30 seconds. It can take a short stroll nearby, pause to investigate and look around. Curiosity uses its existing sniff animation without starting a fossil scan or granting anything.
2. Walk away, change direction and stop. Following uses separate start/stop distances, more frequent path updates and slower speed near you. Vanilla navigation still handles terrain and obstacles.
3. Hold a mineral in either hand. Your companion watches you with interest without consuming the item or changing stats. Put it away to let normal idle behavior resume.
4. Activate the scanner while holding a mineral or during an idle action. Scanning takes priority and holds the original position.
5. Compare Komainu with Segu or Spiko. The latter move and investigate more slowly, while the more active species have shorter intervals.
6. Look at models with a separate head bone: gaze follows the player more clearly, with subtle resting motion and a small interested head tilt. Rigs without a head bone retain their authored animations and whole-body orientation. Reduced-motion preferences suppress the extra decorative head motion.

## Toggle and compatibility

- World/server option: `companions.livingCompanions` in `serverconfig/spectrobesreforged-server.toml`, enabled by default. It is synchronized to clients. Turn it off to compare the previous following/idle behavior. Goal arbitration and scanner-priority fixes remain.
- Same experimental JAR on server and clients. Network protocol **7** prevents mixing it with protocol-6 builds.
- Curios remains optional; inventory/offhand Prizmod use continues to work.
- No new model, texture, advancement, XP, stat, evolution or item-consumption changes. Gesture state is transient and is not stored in the collection.
- Original artist animations and textures remain intact. This is a first behavioral pass, not a complete species animation rework.

## Feedback

Focus on head rotation, pacing, whether curiosity feels too frequent, whether following feels too distant, and any sliding or odd animation transitions. Automated dedicated-server checks do not replace visual testing in Minecraft.

Build with:

```text
.\gradlew.bat "-Pmod_version=0.7.1-beta.1-living-playtest" test build runGameTestServer
```
