# Spectrobes Reforged 0.7.0-beta — Excavation Update

Changes since **0.6.1-beta**. Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.219+**, GeckoLib **4.8.3+**, Java **21**. Curios **9.5.1+1.21.1** is optional.

## Excavation

- Fossil and mineral ores now yield raw specimens to clean at the excavation table. Existing identified fossils remain usable.
- A central excavation board with depth layers, distinct terrain textures, debris, dust, integrity and clear extraction/breakage feedback.
- Ten excavation tools, including drills, blower, recovery, piton, fire/water drills, enhanced blower, vacuum, Giga and Blast, with terrain-specific uses.
- Excavation progress persists when closing the interface, moving the specimen or using another table. Destroyed specimens are lost; their species cannot be rerolled by reopening.
- A new directional table model displays the specimen on its surface. Finished output has display priority; queued raw input waits until output is collected.
- Cooperative excavation with shared progress, remote cursors, names and tool reservations. Different tools can be used simultaneously; the same tool cannot be used by two operators at once.
- One extracted item. Eligible contributors share normal excavation XP and applicable advancements without duplicating the output.
- Perfect and cooperative medals persist on cleaned objects and awakened Spectrobes. The cooperative medal is cosmetic.

## Collection and progression

- Excavation integrity grants a small quality bonus, up to 3% for the highest quality bracket.
- Spectrobe levels and XP up to level 100, mineral feeding progression, saved individual quality and medals. Evolution remains under development.
- Redesigned Prizmod with search, filters, sorting, collection pages, feeding controls, clearer stats and an animated 3D preview.
- All 22 Child species represented by the existing fossils have data. Six retain their own designs; 16 use independent temporary model, texture and animation files ready for replacement.
- New excavation and training advancements. Existing acquisition milestones recognize the original and environmental raw items.
- Child companions cannot take combat or environmental damage. Previously injured Child entries recover when synchronized with the Prizmod.

## World and encounters

- Fossil/mineral deposits in blue ice, sandstone, red sandstone and mud, with biome-appropriate patches and matching raw items.
- Natural frozen fossils remain Flash, desert fossils Corona and swamp/mangrove fossils Aurora. Extra Creative/datapack combinations do not expand natural spawning.
- Revised biome-based vortex encounters with more frequent checks and faster pursuit.
- Cooperative vortex raids with a vanilla-style boss bar and three Krawl per participant. Nearby players can join, with larger but capped personal completion rewards.
- Persistent participant tracking prevents extra waves or rewards from leaving and rejoining. Death reduces personal rewards; losing all participants ends the raid without loot.
- Arrows bounce off idle vortices with shield feedback.

## Awakening, equipment and HUD

- Polished incubator interface with sound-source feedback, signal meter, color bands, countdown and clearer statuses. Uses Minecraft sounds, not a microphone.
- Optional Curios slot for all Prizmod colors. Without Curios, inventory/offhand use and the configured Prizmod shortcut still work. Empty slots now show a neutral placeholder.
- New companion HUD with level, XP, medals and six prepared Adult/Evolved slots.
- Visual HUD editor: drag to move, scroll or use − / + to resize, horizontal/vertical layouts, toggle visibility/team slots and reset. Position is resolution-independent, size ranges from 50–150%, default 85%, and settings persist locally.
- Handheld blower: hold right-click to push mobs in a five-block forward cone. Air is blocked by solid walls. 256 durability, one point per burst every five ticks; no direct damage or player pushing. Creative use is free. The excavation minigame is unchanged; the handheld item has no new recipe yet.
- Client preferences for effects, particles, scanner feedback, hints and reduced motion; server settings for encounter balance.
- A dedicated Discord feedback button in mod settings, with link confirmation. During the beta, a local chat invitation also appears once per world/server login, without broadcasting to others or repeating on respawn/dimension travel.

## Wiki and compatibility

- Six-language wiki with recipes and item/block icons, Spectrobe and Krawl galleries, improved navigation, current news and historical guide selection.
- Updated guidance for excavation, progression, equipment, settings, biomes and cooperative play.
- Use this same mod version on server and clients (network protocol 6). Supports integrated single-player, LAN and dedicated servers.
- Existing IDs and collection data are retained. Back up worlds before updating; new terrain deposits require newly generated chunks.
- Adult evolution and expanded adult combat remain future work; prepared slots and evolution data do not enable them.
