# Companion Life — playtest 3: fetching minerals

Version: **0.7.1-beta.3-living-playtest**. Replace the previous playtest JAR; do not install both. Includes the playtest 2 animated-head fix.

## Try it

Equip a Child companion through the Prizmod. Drop one clean feeding mineral nearby with Q and wait for the normal pickup delay. The companion searches within its species' scanning radius, walks to the mineral, picks up **one unit**, and returns toward you with it visible at its animated mouth.

- **Right-click the companion:** retrieve the carried mineral. Only its owner can retrieve it, including while holding another item; this takes priority over feeding or using that item.
- **Ignore it for 30 seconds while near you:** it eats the mineral using normal feeding, stats, XP and advancement rules. The physical item is consumed once, including in Creative. If it would have no effect, it keeps the mineral for retrieval.
- **Show affection:** right-click with an empty hand when it is not carrying anything to trigger happy particles/hearts. Retrieval and eating also produce happy reactions.
- A full inventory leaves the mineral in its mouth and displays a short status message. Interacting resets its patience timer.

## Rules

- Only clean `MineralItem` feeding items are fetched. Raw specimens and Chroma minerals are excluded.
- Only the owner's active, linked Child can fetch. Unlinked summoned entities cannot silently eat items without a collection entry.
- Reserved drops belonging to another player are ignored. Pickup takes one item atomically; overlapping companions cannot claim the same unit.
- Scanner actions interrupt collection and pause the eating timer. Following and collection run on the server; the render layer follows the existing bone transform once.
- Travel time, owner absence/death and unloaded time do not count toward eating. The item and patience progress survive entity save/load.
- Dismissing/replacing the companion, or removing it for an encounter, releases its carried mineral as an owner-reserved drop rather than losing it.

## Settings and compatibility

World/server config, `companions` group:

- `livingCompanions`: experimental life behavior.
- `fetchMinerals`: enable fetching and unattended eating (default true).
- `mineralPatienceSeconds`: waiting time near the owner (default 30; range 10–300).

Network protocol 7. Same playtest JAR on clients and server. Curios remains optional.

Dedicated-server checks cover real navigation, reservations, single-unit pickup, retrieval precedence, full inventory, scanner priority, NBT persistence, eating exactly once, and refund on dismissal. Mouth placement and reaction feel still need visual testing in Minecraft.
