# Excavation playtest - 0.6.2-beta.1

Private experimental build for gameplay feedback; not a published release.

## Quick start

Use a separate test world and install only this Spectrobes Reforged JAR, with NeoForge 21.1.219+ and GeckoLib 4.8.3+ for Minecraft 1.21.1 / Java 21. Existing clean fossils still work; no world migration is required.

With cheats/operator permissions:

```mcfunction
/give @s spectrobesreforged:excavation_table
/give @s spectrobesreforged:raw_corona_fossil
```

Place the table, right-click it and place the raw fossil in its left input slot. With the scanner [R] active, the cyan silhouette shows where the fossil lies. Select Drill [1] and hold/drag the left mouse button to remove stone. Stop drilling a location once the fossil is exposed: repeated contact damages it. Select Blower [2] to safely remove the last thin rock layer and dusty fragments. The larger drill clears nearby rock even when its center overlaps exposed fossil. Damage starts after twelve idle strokes over exposed fossil and then repeats every four strokes for two integrity points. The percentage counts clean, exposed fossil cells, not the whole board. The controls are buttons and keys local to this interface; tools are included in the table.

At 90% or higher and with integrity remaining, press Extract and take the identified fossil from the output slot next to the input. It works in the existing incubator. You can continue to 100% for a perfect attempt. Crown requires 100%, no damage during the attempt and no more than 60 seconds of active table time. The crown is recorded in the fossil tooltip and has no evolution/stat effect in this prototype. It does not grant Minecraft XP or replace the existing vortex rewards.

Closing pauses the active timer and saves progress; opening it again never resets it. Removing the raw fossil saves the same progress on that item; putting it in another table resumes it. Breaking a table drops its specimen/output. One player can use each table at a time; the server validates work actions, distance and extraction, and limits work to one action every two ticks. At zero integrity the raw specimen is consumed and the fossil is lost.

## Survival

Mine a fossil ore with an iron pickaxe or better, without Silk Touch, to obtain a raw fossil of that element. Silk Touch still drops the ore block. The specimen type is fixed by the loot roll; closing or moving the raw item cannot reroll it. Creative raw items without stored species choose once when inserted into a table.

For this first test the natural pool contains only the six implemented/awakenable species:

- Corona: Komainu, Inkana, Vilar (equal weights).
- Aurora: Segu, Spiko (equal weights).
- Flash: Shakin.

Other already-collected clean fossil items remain unchanged and collectible, but their absent species data is not implemented by this feature. There is no furnace/smelting shortcut for raw fossils.

Table recipe:

```text
I C I
P G P
P - P
```

I = iron ingot (2), C = copper ingot (1), P = any planks (4), G = glass block (1). One table, no redstone/fuel required. The recipe book unlocks after obtaining iron. Table and all three raw items are also in the tools Creative tab.

## Feedback checks

1. Excavate a Corona specimen, extract at 90% and awaken it.
2. Try clearing everything to 100% with no damage and within a minute: is it enjoyable and is a crown achievable?
3. Drill exposed fossil until it breaks. Does the feedback make the mistake understandable?
4. Close halfway, reopen, then move the raw item to another table: progress, damage and elapsed time must persist.
5. Shift-click the specimen into/out of a table; break a table holding one; save/reload. No duplicate fossil or reset should appear.
6. Try extracting with a full output slot or below 90%: it must not consume the raw item.
7. Test table ownership and distance in multiplayer when available.
8. Tell me whether the drill is too fast, the fossil too fragile, dust too obstructive, the board too small, or the scanner too revealing.

## Scope

Implemented: sprite-based silhouettes, randomized stone depth, drill, blower, optional scanner, damage/destruction, saved state, 90% extraction, informational crown, raw drops, crafting and six languages. The working surface is 128 pixels wide with broader tool coverage.

Not implemented in this prototype: separate excavation skill/XP, Recovery tool, ice/lava/water/mud terrain, purchasable tools, Giga/Blast tools or crown-driven evolution. No microphone is needed. This is a layered 2D excavation board, not a freely rotatable 3D fossil.
