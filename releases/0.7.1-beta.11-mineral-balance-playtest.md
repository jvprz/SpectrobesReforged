# Mineral progression balance — playtest 11

- Mineral XP percentage now scales with level, interpolating linearly through efficiency anchors: 1=100%, 25=80%, 50=60%, 75=45%, 100=35%.
- A 10% base mineral grants about 6% at level 50. XP is rounded upward to the nearest integer, retaining usefulness for small rewards.
- A mineral crossing a level boundary spends its remaining percentage budget at the new level’s efficiency. Level 100 remains capped and receives no additional XP.
- All feeding routes share this calculation: direct interaction, Prizmod feeding and companion auto-eating.
- Stat effects, mineral consumption/count, evolution requirements, quality bonuses and combat XP are unchanged. Existing levels and stored XP are preserved; no migration is needed.
- Tooltips distinguish the base percentage from effective XP. Current wiki guides updated in all six languages; historical editions untouched.
- Balance anchors are centralized in SpectrobeLevels for future tuning. This is a Minecraft balance adaptation, rather than a reproduction of Beyond the Portals’ fixed mineral percentages.
