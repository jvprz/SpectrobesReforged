# Komainu — playtest 6

Version: **0.7.1-beta.6-komainu-playtest**. Use the same JAR on clients and server. Canonical release version remains unchanged. Changes in this trial apply only to Komainu.

- Uses the new artist-authored model, base texture and nine animations. Original animation data and base texture are preserved.
- Separate walking and running with a stable speed threshold; sniffing retains scanner priority.
- Curiosity has time to finish; interest holds the authored seated pose without independent head tracking twisting it.
- Independent eye-only blinking, including occasional double blinks.
- Eating pauses navigation. Carrying uses normal movement, with the mineral attached to the artist’s `item` bone under `mouth`.
- Both recolors follow the updated handmade atlas without changing pixels, UVs, transparency or design.
- New positional mono cry derived from the supplied recording, with gentle pitch/volume variations for ambient calls and reactions. A shared cooldown prevents overlapping repeated calls.
- Gallery previews and notes updated in all six wiki languages.

## Try in Minecraft

Observe idle and blinking, walk away slowly and then run, offer a mineral, wait for curiosity, let Komainu fetch a mineral, retrieve it with right click and let it eat another. Check all three colors and the mouth attachment during movement. Cry timing, animation feel and attachment placement still need visual/in-game feedback.

## Artist workflow

Edit the `item` bone pivot to reposition the carried mineral. Keep `item` parented to `mouth`. Blink must affect only `eyes`; the separate controller layers it over other animations. The animation-pass generator now preserves this handmade Komainu animation set. Re-run `tools/art/recolor_komainu.py` after changing the base atlas to regenerate palette-only variants.
