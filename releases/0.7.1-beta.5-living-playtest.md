# Companion Life — playtest 5: animations and medal layout

Version: **0.7.1-beta.5-living-playtest**. Replace playtest 4. Same JAR on clients and server; protocol **8** prevents mixing older trials with the new reaction state.

## Shakin and natural movement

- Shakin's fins now inherit body motion; the fish turns toward its travel direction instead of swimming sideways. Rig pivots are centered on the fish. Cube geometry and UVs remain unchanged.
- Replaced Shakin's walking-like fin flaps with smaller swimming, hovering and scanning motions.
- Calmer idle behavior for Komainu, Spiko, Vilar and Segu; smaller arm/hand excursions for Segu and Inkana; softer hover, leg, ear and tail movement for Zoza while preserving its authored assembled pose.
- Added five contextual clips per custom species: curiosity, interest, carrying, happiness and eating. They are driven by existing behavior, pickup/retrieval, petting and feeding, rather than playing randomly all the time.
- Existing scanner animations retain priority. The previous animated-head/pivot fix remains. Original artist animations are backed up in `art/animation-pass/source/`.
- Temporary models retain their existing generic clips. Textures and sprites are unchanged.

## Prizmod and medals

- Collection cards reserve a separate area for medals and level, so two medals do not cover the portrait.
- Details show medals in dedicated slots beside the 3D preview. Hover for the medal name and “Obtained by…” explanation.
- Companion/team strips use their own badge positions; HUD medals sit in the header rather than after the name.
- Client setting **Show medals beside Spectrobe names** is available under **Mods → Spectrobes Reforged → Config → Mod preferences**. It changes local nameplate/name display only, preserving saved medals and their separate badges.
- Labels, tooltips and the setting are translated in all six languages.
