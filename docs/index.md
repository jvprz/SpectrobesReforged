# Your next discovery starts here

Explore the world, uncover ancient fossils, and awaken a companion of your own. This is the player wiki for **Spectrobes Reforged <!-- VERSION -->0.6.1-beta<!-- /VERSION -->**, for **Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge**.

> **Our first beta: a growing exploration and encounter experience.** This guide describes the bundled code and data. Custom modpacks and datapacks can change the experience. Planned features are marked explicitly.

## Begin your journey

1. [Install the mod and its dependencies](getting-started.md).
2. [Find fossil ores in the right biomes](fossils.md).
3. [Power an incubator and awaken a supported fossil](awakening.md).
4. [Manage your companion in the Prizmod](prizmod.md).
5. [Scan for buried discoveries](scanning.md) and [feed minerals](minerals.md).

**New in this beta:** [Prizmod and incubator crafting recipes](getting-started.md#craft-your-equipment), [advancements](advancements.md), and pursuing [Krawl vortices](vortices.md).

## What works today?

| System | Current state |
| --- | --- |
| Fossil and mineral ores | Biome-based fossil generation; mineral generation in the Overworld |
| Awakening | Redstone-powered incubator, sound input, three color variants |
| Collection | Persistent storage, one child companion slot, six team slots |
| Companion | Following and an underground scan |
| Feeding | HP, attack and defense adjustments; Chroma recoloring |
| Krawl | JSON definitions, animated entities and spawn eggs |
| Evolution | Targets and mineral thresholds exist; no completed evolution interaction |
| Team combat | Slots and stats exist; a complete deployment/battle loop is not implemented |
| Analysis and cleaning | Not implemented |
